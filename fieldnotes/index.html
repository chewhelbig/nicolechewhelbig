FieldNotes runs entirely in this page. Your key and everything you write go straight from your browser to Anthropic (Claude) and back — never through any server. Your key is kept only in this browser, on this device, until you remove it.

A word on confidentiality. What you type about a client is sent to Anthropic to be processed, so write the least that lets you think — a code or initials rather than full names. Nothing is stored by FieldNotes; close or clear the page and it's gone.
